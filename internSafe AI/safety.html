BEFORE YOU APPLY

Your quick verification checklist.

  • Find the role on the company's official careers page.
  • Verify the recruiter through a known company contact.
  • Check the company name, email domain, and job details.
  • Do not rely on HTTPS or a low risk score alone.

Can a score prove fraud?

No. It can only support a decision when backed by reliable checks and clear limitations.

Does HTTPS mean a site is safe?

No. HTTPS encrypts the connection; it does not prove a company or job is legitimate.
