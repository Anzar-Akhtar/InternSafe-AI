THE IDEA

Helping students make
more informed choices.

InternSafe AI is a student project concept focused on spotting suspicious signs in job and internship opportunities.

OUR PURPOSE

Make the first check simpler.

Recruitment scams can use urgent language, suspicious payment requests, and misleading details. The project aims to make it easier to pause and review those signals.

It is designed to support awareness, not replace a person's judgment or official company verification.
